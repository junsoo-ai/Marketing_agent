## SECTION 1 — GLOBAL EVENTS

**Bitcoin ETF Bleeding Deepens as Nine-Day Outflow Streak Hits $223M Single-Day Exit**

Despite this sustained institutional exit pressure, BTC prices recovered sharply to $60,751 (+3.6% 24h), suggesting spot buyers and derivatives positioning are temporarily absorbing the ETF-driven selling. **U.S. Inflation Remains Sticky, Keeping Fed Rate Cuts Off the Table**
The Philadelphia Fed's Q2 2026 Survey of Professional Forecasters revised headline CPI inflation sharply upward to 6.0% annualized for the current quarter, up from a prior estimate of 2.7%, while core CPI was revised to 3.2% from 2.8%. May 2026 CPI data released in June showed prices rising 4.2% annually, driven largely by a 3.9% monthly surge in energy costs that pushed the 12-month energy gain to 23.5%. The Federal Reserve remains firmly on hold, with consumers still expecting elevated inflation through year-end, suppressing any near-term dovish pivot. *(Philadelphia Fed SPF Q2 2026; CNBC, June 10 2026; New York Times, June 10 2026)*

**Dow Posts Back-to-Back Losses as S&P 500 Slides to 7,475 on Weak ADP Data**
The Dow Jones Industrial Average closed at 52,088.33 on July 1, down 230.87 points (−0.44%), extending recent softness in U.S. equities. The S&P 500 declined approximately 0.32% to the 7,475 level, while ADP private payrolls for June came in at 98,000 — well below the 117,000 consensus forecast and down from 122,000 in May — signaling a cooling labor market ahead of Friday's official NFP report. The Nasdaq was broadly weaker alongside the broader market, though earlier-in-the-week data showed the index had shed 1.32% to 26,166.60 on June 22. *(WSJ Market Data, July 1 2026; Trading Economics, July 1 2026; CNN Markets Economic Calendar, July 1 2026)*

**Gold Rises to $4,061 Amid Lingering Middle East Risk Premium**
Gold climbed to $4,061.30 per troy ounce on July 1, 2026, a gain of 1.34% on the day, as geopolitical uncertainty stemming from Middle East tensions continued to support safe-haven demand. Despite the daily bounce, gold has fallen 9.55% over the past month, suggesting broader risk-off unwinding remains incomplete. Petrobras CEO Magda Chambriard noted that oil prices appear to be stabilizing in the $72–$75 per barrel range, reflecting markets that are not fully normalized, a backdrop that keeps the geopolitical risk premium alive across commodities. *(Trading Economics, July 1 2026; Economic Times / ET Markets, July 1 2026)*

**Hyperliquid Open Interest Crosses $10 Billion as Platform Expands Into Stocks and Commodities**
Decentralized derivatives exchange Hyperliquid surpassed $10 billion in open interest for perpetual futures contracts as of July 1, 2026, a milestone driven partly by its HIP-3 upgrade enabling community-deployed perp DEXs for non-crypto assets including equities and commodities. BTC perpetual funding rates on Hyperliquid averaged approximately 0.00135% over the past six months, ranking among the most competitive for long-position holders across major venues. The platform's TVL figures show $4.7 billion on the Hyperliquid Bridge and $1.5 billion on its L1 layer, underscoring deepening liquidity concentration on-chain. *(Crowdfund Insider, June 2026; CryptoRank, July 1 2026; Hyperliquid Docs / eco.com, May 2026)*
